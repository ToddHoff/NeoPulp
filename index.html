A book format
Somebody had to think of it. That was the hard part.
A neopulp is a book whose premise, structure and ending belong to a person, and whose sentences were written by a machine at that person's instruction. The idea is the authorship. It always was.

The promise every neopulp makes
01
Idea-first fiction
One premise, taken seriously, followed to the end. Not a genre — a way of deciding what a book is for. The concept carries the weight the prose style used to.
02
Said out loud
Every neopulp names how it was made, on the title page, in the same size as the byline. Not a disclaimer at the back. A credit at the front.
03
Nobody's brand
There's no publisher here. The mark is free to apply to your own book if you keep the promise and print the credit. That's the whole membership test.
“It's a
neopulp.”
Lowercase, with an article, like a novel, a zine, a podcast. Plural: neopulps. The word is a common noun and belongs to everyone using it — that's the point, and it's why nobody trademarks it.

Which file, and when
STAMP-FULL
Back covers, title pages, print at 34mm and up. The only version that carries the promise.
STAMP-COMPACT
22–34mm. Front covers with room to spare. Promise drops, disclosure stays.
STAMP-BADGE
Under 22mm. Spines, thumbnails, busy covers, storefront listings.
SEAL-SQUARE
Avatars, favicons, app tiles, die-cut stickers. Never on a cover.
WORDMARK-BLACK
The word itself, light backgrounds. For writing about the format — sites, posters, decks.
WORDMARK-REVERSED
Dark backgrounds. NEO turns white, PULP stays black on acid — that pairing never inverts.
WORDMARK-MONO
One-ink print, foil, emboss, copyright pages. No highlight available at all.

SVG or PNG?
SVG for anything printed or resized. PNG at 4× for slides, social, and storefronts that reject vectors. Never scale a PNG up.
Before the printer
Convert type to outlines. Widths are pinned in the SVG so lockups hold their proportions even where Archivo isn't installed — but outlines are safer.
Typefaces
Archivo and JetBrains Mono, both open-licence and free. Your book's interior can be set in anything; only the mark is fixed.
