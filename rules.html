Mark rules & specification

How to use the mark

Neopulp is a movement, not a publisher. The mark is free to apply to your own book. Keep the promise, print the credit, and don't redraw the mark — everything below is the detail of those three rules.

01 · Ink

Two inks and a stock. No third colour.

ValueNameUse
#0D0C0BPress blackType, rules, stamp keyline
#E8FF4DSignal acidHighlight and seed square only — never as type colour, never a field larger than the stamp

No tints, no gradients, no glow, no drop shadows.

02 · Type

Archivo and JetBrains Mono. Nothing else.

FaceWhere

JetBrains MonoNumbering, disclosure, all letterspaced small text

Both are open-licence and free. Your book's interior can be set in anything — only the mark is fixed.

03 · The seven rules

What holds the mark together

  1. One word. NEOPULP — never spaced, never camel-cased, never hyphenated. Lowercase “neopulp” in running text, because it is a common noun.
  2. The highlight sits behind PULP only. The insult is the part that gets highlighted. Behind the whole word it becomes decoration and the argument disappears.
  3. Clear space equals the cap-height of the wordmark, on all four sides. Nothing enters it.
  4. Minimum sizes. Stamp 22mm wide in print, 96px on screen. Below that, use the badge. Wordmark never below 11px.
  5. The acid square is fixed. Same size relative to cap-height, always top-right of the stamp, always keylined.
  6. Never rotate the stamp. It is a certification, not a sticker.
  7. Never lock up with another movement mark. Not the same surface, not the same file.

05 · On someone else's cover

The only flexibility granted

  • The keyline and wordmark may take your cover's text colour.
  • The badge may sit on a solid stock field if your cover is busy — never keyline-only over artwork.
  • Any of the sizes, any corner, any edge.
  • The square seal is fair game on a cover — same corners, same caps. It has no disclosure line, so the title-page credit carries that weight alone.

06 · Locked wording

The credit and the promise

Reader promise — on the stamp, back cover, and storefront line:

A human vision, perfected.

Credit line — title page, at byline scale, above the fold:

Seeded by [name]
Created, shaped and judged by the author. Written with a machine.

The standard behind the mark — agency plus responsibility. Not that a human typed it; that a human answered for it, through every draft:

I created this, I shaped it, I judged it, and I stand behind it.

Typing “write a great novel” and publishing whatever comes back does not meet it. Authorship is the two-hundredth exchange, not the first.

Shelf and search language: idea-first fiction.

Never appears anywhere
“AI-ASSISTED” · “AI-GENERATED”
ANY SENTENCE OPENING “WHILE SOME MAY…”

Saying the machine was a collaborator is not a hedge — it's how the book was made. Hedging is the apology; the credit line is the opposite of one.

07 · The word

What to call it, and why

What it is. “Neopulp” does two jobs. Capitalised, it names the mark and the movement: the Neopulp mark, Neopulp is a movement, not a publisher. Lowercase, it is the common noun for a book that carries the mark — a kind of book, the way a novel, a zine, or a podcast is a kind of thing. It's a neopulp. She's written three neopulps.

Why it matters. A common noun belongs to everyone who uses it. A brand name needs an owner, a ™, and permission — and the whole point of the mark is that nobody owns it and nobody has to ask. A word people can drop into a sentence spreads on its own; a brand name has to be marketed. So the word is deliberately not trademarked, and we ask that nobody else trademark it either.

08 · Series numbering

Optional, and Arabic

№ 004 in JetBrains Mono, at the head of the spine or top-left of the cover. Use it if you're publishing a sequence; skip it if you aren't.

09 · Files

Production notes

  • SVG for anything printed or resized; PNG at 4× for slides, social, and storefronts that reject vectors. Never scale a PNG up.
  • Convert type to outlines before sending to a printer, or install both typefaces.
  • Lockup widths are pinned with textLength, so proportions hold even where Archivo isn't installed.
