A NOTE TO THE READER

THIS BOOK IS A NEOPULP.

Its premise, structure and ending belong to a person. Its sentences were written by a machine at that person's instruction. The idea is the authorship — it always was.

For most of history, ideas died in the gap between imagining a book and having the ten thousand hours to write it. That gap just closed. What's left was always the human part and always the hard part: the idea, the taste, the direction, the nerve to follow one vision all the way to the end.

A book isn't good because a human wrote every word, and it isn't bad because a machine wrote any of them. A book is good or it isn't. You will decide.

A human vision, perfected.
— the promise every neopulp makes
